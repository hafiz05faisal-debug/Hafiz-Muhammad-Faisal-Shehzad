About

I completed my PhD in Information Systems at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia in 2022 as an HEC-funded International Doctoral Fellow. My doctoral research modelled the interoperability factors behind Building Information Modelling adoption in the Malaysian construction industry, published in Computers in Industry and Construction Innovation.

As Chairman, I have led NCEAC accreditation visits for the BSCS, BSIT, BS AI and BS Data Science programmes and the curriculum development for the department's AI and Data Science degrees. In 2025 I served on the HEC National Curriculum Review Committee for Computing.
